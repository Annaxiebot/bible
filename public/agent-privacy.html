隐私声明 · Privacy

superelf agent（个人助理 · personal assistant）· 更新 Updated 2026-10-23

这是什么 · What it is

superelf agent 是本站负责人自用的个人助理，运行在负责人自己的电脑上，不向任何其他人提供服务。superelf agent is a personal assistant that the owner of this site runs for their own use, on their own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

访问什么 · What it accesses

  • 负责人自己的 Gmail 和 Google 云端硬盘，只读（gmail.readonly、drive.readonly），用于整理邮件、提醒和摘要。The owner's own Gmail and Google Drive, read-only (gmail.readonly, drive.readonly), to sort email and to prepare reminders and summaries.
  • 它不能发送、删除、修改或分享任何邮件或文件。It cannot send, delete, change or share any email or file.

数据去向 · Where the data goes

  • 读取的内容保存在负责人自己的电脑上。What it reads stays on the owner's own computer.
  • 处理时，所需的内容可能发给负责人选择的 AI 模型服务。To process it, the needed content may be sent to the AI model service the owner has chosen.
  • 不出售，不用于广告，不与任何人分享。Never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone.

Google 用户数据 · Google user data

本应用对 Google API 所获信息的使用遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的有限使用要求。superelf agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

撤销与联系 · Revoke and contact

随时可以在 Google 账号的「第三方应用和服务」中撤销访问。问题请写信给 csheargm@gmail.com。Access can be revoked at any time under "Third-party apps and services" in the Google Account. Questions: write to the address above.
