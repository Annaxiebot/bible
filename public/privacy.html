隐私声明 · Privacy

Scripture to Life (scripturetolife.org) · 更新 Updated 2026-10-02

我们收集什么 · What we collect

  • 查经组员（扫码报名）：姓名、电邮或手机号、你选择的一周操练。Group members who sign up by QR: name, email or phone, the one weekly practice you chose.
  • 带领者（Google 登录）：Google 账号的电邮和姓名，以及你在本站的设置（AI 模型选择、内容语言）。Leaders who sign in with Google: the email and name of the Google account, plus your settings on this site (AI model choices, content language).
  • 周中提醒的回答：默认只保存在你自己的设备上；只有你选择「分享给带领者」时才会发送给带领者。Mid-week check-in answers stay on your own device by default; they reach the leader only when you choose "Share with leader".

我们如何使用 · How we use it

  • 只用于你报名的查经组的周中提醒（周二、周四、周末各一封）。Only for the mid-week check-ins of the group you signed up for (Tuesday, Thursday, weekend).
  • 不出售，不用于广告，不与查经组之外的任何人分享。Never sold, never used for advertising, never shared outside your group.
  • 「问一问 Ask AI」的问题通过你自己的 OpenRouter 密钥直接发送给模型提供方；本站不保存这些问题。"Ask AI" questions go straight to the model provider through your own OpenRouter key; this site does not store them.

存储与删除 · Storage and deletion

  • 报名、设置和带领者的查经包存放在 Supabase（美国），只有该带领者本人可以读取查经包。Sign-ups, settings and leaders' study packs are stored on Supabase (United States); only the leader can read their own packs.
  • 随时可以要求删除你的全部数据：回复任何一封提醒邮件，或写信给 csheargm@gmail.com。Ask for deletion of all your data at any time: reply to any check-in email, or write to the address above.

Google 登录 · Google sign-in

带领者登录只请求基本身份（电邮、姓名），不请求其他 Google 权限。Leader sign-in asks for basic identity only (email, name) and no other Google permission.

Scripture to Life 是一个服务教会小组查经的非商业项目。Scripture to Life is a non-commercial project serving church small-group Bible study.
