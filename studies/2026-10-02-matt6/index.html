「不要为生命忧虑」 “Do not worry about your life” (v.25)

经文 Scripture
#和合本 CUVBSB
25「所以我告诉你们，不要为生命忧虑吃甚么，喝甚么；为身体忧虑穿甚么。生命不胜于饮食么？身体不胜于衣裳么？Therefore I tell you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat or drink; or about your body, what you will wear. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothes?
26你们看那天上的飞鸟，也不种，也不收，也不积蓄在仓里，你们的天父尚且养活他。你们不比飞鸟贵重得多吗？Look at the birds of the air: They do not sow or reap or gather into barns, and yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?
27你们那一个能用思虑使寿数多加一刻呢〔或作：使身量多加一肘呢〕？Who of you by worrying can add a single hour to his life?
28何必为衣裳忧虑呢？你想野地里的百合花怎么长起来；它也不劳苦，也不纺线。And why do you worry about clothes? Consider how the lilies of the field grow: They do not labor or spin.
29然而我告诉你们，就是所罗门极荣华的时候，他所穿戴的，还不如这花一朵呢！Yet I tell you that not even Solomon in all his glory was adorned like one of these.
30你们这小信的人哪！野地里的草今天还在，明天就丢在炉里，神还给它这样的妆饰，何况你们呢！If that is how God clothes the grass of the field, which is here today and tomorrow is thrown into the furnace, will He not much more clothe you, O you of little faith?
31所以，不要忧虑说：吃甚么？喝甚么？穿甚么？Therefore do not worry, saying, ‘What shall we eat?’ or ‘What shall we drink?’ or ‘What shall we wear?’
32这都是外邦人所求的，你们需用的这一切东西，你们的天父是知道的。For the Gentiles strive after all these things, and your heavenly Father knows that you need them.
33你们要先求他的国和他的义，这些东西都要加给你们了。But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added unto you.
34所以，不要为明天忧虑，因为明天自有明天的忧虑；一天的难处一天当就彀了。」Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself. Today has enough trouble of its own.

背景 Context

  • 属于登山宝训 · Part of the Sermon on the Mount.
  • 第25节的「所以」承接 vv.19–24：积财宝在天上，「一个人不能事奉两个主——神和玛门」 · The “therefore” in v.25 hangs on vv.19–24: treasures in heaven, “no one can serve two masters — God and money.”
  • 耶稣把为吃穿忧虑，与我们信靠谁、事奉谁连在一起 · Jesus connects worry about food and clothing with whom we trust and serve.
  • 这是属灵应用，不是诊断：焦虑不都表示事奉错了主；持续或严重的焦虑，也该寻求医生或辅导 · Spiritual application, not diagnosis: not all anxiety means serving the wrong master; persistent or severe anxiety deserves a doctor’s or counselor’s care too.
  • 经文把吃穿当作真实的需要：「你们的天父是知道的」（v.32） · The text treats food and clothing as real needs: “your heavenly Father knows” (v.32).

原文 Original Language

  • μεριμνάω (merimnaō)：忧虑、挂心；马大「思虑烦扰」（路加福音 10:41）是同一个动词 · merimnaō: to be anxious, to care; Martha’s “worried” (Luke 10:41) is the same verb.
  • 把它连到 μερίζω「分开」、说成「心被撕裂」，是有争议的词源，只宜当图像 · Linking it to merizō (“divide”), a mind “pulled apart”, is debated etymology — an illustration, not a definition.
  • v.25 是现在时命令式，有人读作「不要再忧虑」；但 vv.31、34 是不定过去时。单凭时态定不了，要看上下文 · v.25 is a present imperative, which some read as “stop worrying”; but vv.31 and 34 are aorist. Tense alone cannot settle it — context must.
  • merimnaō 也可以是好的——保罗用它形容提摩太挂念教会（腓立比书 2:20） · Note merimnaō can be good — Paul uses it for Timothy’s care for the church (Philippians 2:20).
  • 问题不在于在不在乎，而在于在乎是否挤掉了对天父的信靠（v.32） · The question is not whether you care, but whether caring crowds out trust in the Father (v.32).

交叉经文 Cross-references

  • 路加福音 12:22–32 · Luke 12:22–32 — 平行经文；第32节：「你们这小群，不要惧怕」 · parallel; v.32: “little flock, fear not”
  • 腓立比书 4:6–7 · Philippians 4:6–7 — 替代之道：祷告加感恩 → 平安 · the replacement: prayer with thanksgiving → peace
  • 彼得前书 5:7 · 1 Peter 5:7 — 「将一切的忧虑卸给神」 · “cast all your anxiety on him”
  • 诗篇 55:22 · Psalm 55:22 — 旧约根源 · OT root
  • 马太福音 6:19–24 · Matthew 6:19–24 — 紧接在前的上下文（第25节「所以」所承接的） · the context immediately before (what the “therefore” in v.25 points back to)

讨论 Discussion

  1. 读 vv.25–34：耶稣叫我们不要为哪些事忧虑？他给了哪些理由？先列出来，暂不解释。 · Read vv.25–34: what does Jesus tell us not to worry about, and what reasons does he give? List them before interpreting.
  2. v.25 的「所以」承接 vv.19–24 的哪些话？v.32 怎么说吃穿这些需要？ · What in vv.19–24 does the “therefore” of v.25 point back to? What does v.32 say about needs like food and clothing?
  3. 耶稣把这段话接在「两个主」（v.24）之后。两者有什么关系？——不必假定每个忧虑都出于错误的忠诚 · Jesus places this right after “two masters” (v.24). How do the two connect? — without assuming every worry comes from misplaced loyalty.
  4. 这一周，忧虑实际出现在哪里？说出最主要的一两个。 · Where does anxiety actually show up in your week? Name the top one or two.
  5. 负责任的计划（经文并不禁止）与耶稣所说的忧虑，具体差别在哪里？ · What is the difference, concretely, between responsible planning (which the passage does not forbid) and the worry Jesus describes?
  6. v.33「先求他的国」紧接着 v.32「天父是知道的」。在你刚才说的处境里，「先求」会是什么样子？ · v.33 “seek first the kingdom” follows v.32 “your heavenly Father knows.” In the situation you named, what would “seeking first” look like?
  7. 腓立比书 4:6–7 给出替代的习惯。本周你真能持守的一个操练是什么？从生活应用菜单里选一个，并告诉一个人你选了哪个。 · Philippians 4:6–7 gives the replacement habit. What is one practice you could actually keep this week? Pick it from the Life menu and tell one person which you chose.

生活应用 Life Menu

健康 Health固定的睡前程序：放下手机 + 读太6:34 + 定点熄灯，坚持五晚 · Fixed wind-down: phone away + Matthew 6:34 + lights out at a set time, 5 nights.
关系 Relationships把你最大的忧虑告诉一位信任的人，请他为此祷告一次 · Tell one trusted person your top anxiety; ask them to pray for it once.
家庭 Family一顿饭的时间，每人说一次神在忧虑中供应的经历 · One meal where each person names a time God provided when they worried.
工作 Work把忧虑写下来，标注「今天的还是明天的？」（v.34），把明天的留在纸上 · Write the worry down, mark “today’s or tomorrow’s?” (v.34), park tomorrow’s on paper.
情绪 Emotional腓4:6 反应操练：忧虑一起来，就说一句祈求加一句感恩 · Philippians 4:6 reflex: one sentence of request + one of thanksgiving each time anxiety rises.
财务 Finance检视一项为了安抚忧虑而存在的固定开支，做个决定 · Review one recurring expense that exists to quiet anxiety; decide about it.
属灵 Spiritual每天用 v.33 祷告：「今天先求神的国是什么样子？」——每早写下一行 · Pray v.33 daily: “what does seeking first look like today?” — one written line each morning.
